Doctor: Good morning Sam, thanks for meeting with us. Present today are me, the registrar Priya and your nurse Tom.
Sam: Morning. Okay.
Doctor: How have the last few days been on the ward?
Sam: Better than last week. I've been sleeping about six hours a night now.
Doctor: Last week you told us the voices were very loud. How are they now?
Sam: They're quieter. Mostly at night when it's quiet. They just comment on what I'm doing.
Doctor: Are they telling you to do anything, like hurt yourself or anyone else?
Sam: No, not anymore. They used to tell me to stop eating but they don't now.
Doctor: And how is your mood, on a scale of zero to ten?
Sam: About a six. Last week it was a two.
Doctor: Any thoughts of ending your life or harming yourself?
Sam: No. I want to get back home to my daughter. She's seven.
Tom: Nursing notes say Sam has been on 15/60 observations, attending meals and the group in the afternoon. No incidents since Thursday.
Doctor: Thanks Tom. Sam, you're on olanzapine fifteen milligrams at night. How are you finding it?
Sam: It's okay but I'm hungry all the time. I've put on weight I think.
Tom: His weight is up two kilos since admission.
Doctor: We'll check your bloods this week, fasting glucose and lipids, and keep an eye on the weight.
Doctor: Do you still think people at your old job were trying to poison you?
Sam: I'm not sure anymore. Maybe I was unwell. My sister thinks I was.
Doctor: That's an important change. Have you used any cannabis or alcohol since you came in?
Sam: No. Before I came in I was smoking cannabis every day, maybe two grams.
Doctor: You're under the Mental Health Act as an involuntary patient. Do you understand why?
Sam: Sort of. Because I wasn't safe and I wasn't eating.
Doctor: Your sister rang yesterday. She said you sounded much more like yourself.
Doctor: My plan is to continue olanzapine fifteen milligrams at night and not change it this week.
Doctor: We'll start escorted ground leave, thirty minutes twice a day, with nursing staff.
Doctor: The dietitian will see you about the appetite, and the social worker will call your sister about the family meeting next Tuesday.
Doctor: You'll stay on 15/60 observations for now, and we'll review again on Friday.
Sam: Okay. Thank you.
